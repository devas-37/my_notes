# 74HC595N

![CleanShot 2026-11-18 at 17.48.28.png](<../assets/6d736182-da3d-4f4c-b603-82f8402a0343-CleanShot-2026-11-18-at-17-48-28.png>)

74HC595 — ketma-ket kirishni sakkizta parallel chiqishga aylantiradigan shift registr. Ichidagi alohida saqlash registri (latch) yangi bitlar yuklanayotganda chiqishlarni o‘zgarmas tutadi. LED va raqamli indikatorlarni kam GPIO bilan boshqarishda ishlatiladi.

TI’ning **SN74HC595N** modelida `N` — PDIP-16 korpus. Quyidagi IC blok shu 16-pin joylashuv uchun. [TI datasheet, PDF](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74hc595.pdf).

## IC: DIP-16

Yuqoridan ko‘rinish, o‘yiq tepada. `/` — faol-past signal. Q0–Q7 boshqa datasheetlarda QA–QH deb yoziladi.

```ic{DIP-16} 74HC595N
1: Q1 / QB
2: Q2 / QC
3: Q3 / QD
4: Q4 / QE
5: Q5 / QF
6: Q6 / QG
7: Q7 / QH
8: GND
9: Q7S / QH'
10: /MR / /SRCLR
11: SHCP / SRCLK
12: STCP / RCLK
13: /OE
14: DS / SER
15: Q0 / QA
16: VCC
```

Pin nomlari: [TI, 3-bet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74hc595.pdf#page=3) va [Nexperia, 5-bet](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74HC_HCT595.pdf#page=5).

## Boshqaruv

| Pin | Signal | Vazifasi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 14 | DS / SER | Ketma-ket ma’lumot |
| 11 | SHCP / SRCLK | Ko‘tariluvchi frontda bitlarni siljitadi |
| 12 | STCP / RCLK | Ko‘tariluvchi frontda qiymatni latch’ga ko‘chiradi |
| 13 | /OE | LOW: chiqishlar faol; HIGH: yuqori impedans |
| 10 | /MR / /SRCLR | LOW: shift registrni asinxron tozalaydi |
| 9 | Q7S / QH′ | Keyingi mikrosxemaga ketma-ket chiqish |

`/MR` latch’ni tozalamaydi. Chiqishlarni nol qilish uchun shift registrni tozalab, keyin STCP impulsini bering. `/OE` registrdagi qiymatni o‘zgartirmaydi. [Nexperia datasheet](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74HC_HCT595.pdf).

## Bayt yuborish

1. `/MR = HIGH`, STCP = LOW qiling.
2. DS’ga bitni qo‘ying; SHCP’ga LOW → HIGH impulsi bering.
3. Sakkiz bit uchun takrorlang.
4. STCP’ga LOW → HIGH impulsi bering: chiqishlar birgalikda yangilanadi.
5. `/OE = LOW` qilib chiqishlarni yoqing.

**MSB-first** yuborilganda `bit0 → Q0`, `bit7 → Q7`. Masalan, `10000001` Q0 va Q7’ni HIGH qiladi. Bu ketma-ketlik datasheetdagi siljitish va latch qoidalaridan kelib chiqadi.

Kaskad uchun birinchi chipning Q7S pinini ikkinchisining DS piniga ulang; SHCP va STCP umumiy bo‘ladi. Ikki chipga 16 bit yuborib, bitta latch impulsi bering. [Nexperia PDF](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74HC_HCT595.pdf).

## Amaliy ulash

- VCC: **2–6 V**, GND: umumiy yer.
- VCC–GND orasiga chip yonida **100 nF** bypass kondensator qo‘ying.
- Har bir LED uchun alohida tok cheklovchi rezistor ishlating.
- Tokni chiqish va butun chip limitlari bo‘yicha hisoblang; absolute maximum qiymatlarni ishchi maqsad sifatida olmang.
- HC va HCT kirish chegaralari farq qiladi; 3.3 V MCU bilan 5 V HC kirishining HIGH shartini tekshiring.

Elektrik chegaralar va bypass: [TI datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74hc595.pdf). HC/HCT farqi: [Nexperia datasheet](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74HC_HCT595.pdf).
